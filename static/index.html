The problem

A city average hides the street that is in danger.

Heat harms people unevenly. Two wards in the same city can face very different exposure, tree cover and health capacity on the same afternoon. A single citywide forecast cannot tell a health officer where to send help first.

Risk is local

Each ward gets its own score from hazard, vulnerability, exposure and adaptive capacity, so hot spots stand out instead of averaging away.

Air temperature is not the whole story

Humidity, wind and solar radiation decide how hot the body feels. ThermaLens reports the Universal Thermal Climate Index alongside air temperature.

Warnings must be understood to work

Alerts go out by SMS and WhatsApp in English, Hindi and the local state language, with clear steps and an emergency line.

Alerts

Clear guidance, in the language people read.

When a ward escalates, ThermaLens composes a short message with the band, the thermal reading and what to do next. Messages are delivered over SMS and WhatsApp through Twilio.

  • Personal guidance. Practical steps such as staying indoors in the afternoon and drinking water every hour.
  • Local language. English and Hindi, plus the state language of each pilot city.
  • An honest log. The alert log lists what was actually queued or sent. Examples are never mixed in.

Questions

What people ask before they rely on it.

Is the data live?
Each city reports whether its weather is live or a fallback, and the status pill in the dashboard header says which. Fallback data is always labelled and never presented as live.
What does a heatwave preview do?
It re-runs the model on temperatures you raise, from 0 to 10 °C. Results are marked PREVIEW everywhere and are never recorded as observations. Return to live data at any time.
Are alerts really sent?
Alerts are delivered over SMS and WhatsApp through Twilio. The alert log shows everything the system has queued or sent. The message-format examples are illustrations and were never sent.
Does it cover all of India?
No. Ward-level modelling covers four pilot cities. The national map highlights the pilot states and does not suggest ward coverage elsewhere.
How trustworthy are the health layers?
The calibration basis is shown per city in the ward panel. Hospitalisation coefficients are defaults unless a city shows a fitted basis, and the interface says so wherever it applies.
